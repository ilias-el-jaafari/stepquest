# OpenStreetMap, Overpass and OpenFreeMap etiquette

StepQuest runs no server. The map is **drawn** from OpenFreeMap vector tiles (a free CDN, no key), and the **walking graph** comes from the same tiles (z14 `transportation`, task 78) or the bundled extract; public Overpass instances run by volunteers only **enrich** that graph with lit, sidewalk and name tags. Both are shared, free services, so the client must stay polite. Overpass rules are implemented in `src/osm/overpass.ts` and `src/osm/download.ts`, tile rules in `src/vector/client.ts` and `src/vector/fetchTile.ts`, and both lanes are scheduled by `src/world/streamer.ts`; keep them intact. The reasons for the numbers are in `.kiro/specs/map-rendering/design.md` ("Root cause", "Map loading v2", "Map data from vector tiles"); change a limit only together with that section.

## Overpass (enrichment of the walking graph)

Never draw from Overpass and never make the drawing wait for it (requirement 21.1): at peak hours the public servers answer 502 "at capacity" or nothing at all.

- **Cache first.** Look in IndexedDB (7-day TTL, `CACHE_TTL_MS`) and in the area index for a cached area that already covers the request before any network call. Cache keys include the codec version and grow the box outwards to a 0.001° grid (about 100 m) so nearby requests share an entry and an entry always covers its key; the grown box is what is downloaded and what the size cap measures. Expired, evicted and older-version entries are deleted locally after a save; this never sends a request. A damaged entry counts as a miss.
- **Two requests at most per tab, spaced.** `MAX_IN_FLIGHT` (2) downloads at once, starts at least `MIN_REQUEST_GAP_MS` (1.5 s) apart, and at most 12 starts in any 60 s (`MAX_REQUESTS_PER_MINUTE`). A third is refused (`busy`), not queued; an explicit "load this area" download runs alone. Why not 1 and 10 s as before: Overpass limits each IP by slots (typically two on the main servers; `/api/status` shows it) and answers 429 when they are taken, so two is what a polite client may hold; one request with a 10 s gap made a 12-cell view take 30 s at best (design.md, Root cause). This doubles the worst-case request rate (6 to 12 a minute) and raises the cells per request from 4 to 6, so the real bound is demand: a cold quest start needs about 6 requests, a 90-minute walk about 10 more, and a second visit nothing for 7 days (cache). Revisit if the mirrors start answering 429 to normal use.
- **Size cap.** Refuse areas above `MAX_AREA_KM2` (36 km²). Ask only for the area a walk needs (`areaForWalk`), and `out geom` for ways only: highways, buildings, parks and green landuse, water, waterways. Relations are not requested (bigger answers); the parser reads multipolygon relations if an extract has them, so adding them to the query is an owner decision.
- **Mirrors, hedged.** The public mirrors in `OVERPASS_ENDPOINTS` are asked in order, the last good one first. The next mirror is asked only when the one before failed or has sent no answer header for `HEDGE_MS` (6 s); the first good header wins and the others are aborted before they send a body (never three bodies over one phone link, never three times the load on volunteers). Limits: header 25 s (`HEADER_TIMEOUT_MS`), stall 12 s (`STALL_TIMEOUT_MS`), one call 60 s (`TOTAL_TIMEOUT_MS`), the client deadline 75 s (`REQUEST_DEADLINE_MS`) that frees the slot and ends the worker. Answer 429 and 504 as `rateLimited`. An answer without an `elements` array is `badResponse` and a timeout remark is `busy`; neither is cached. Do not add mirrors that forbid this use.
- **Back-off with jitter.** A failed graph cell waits 4 s ±25 %, doubling to 2 min; a 429 or 504 pauses every start for at least 20 s ±25 %. Jitter so many phones do not retry together. No unit is given up for good while the view needs it, and a failing unit never delays another. Why shorter than the old 30 s to 5 min: on a mobile link most failures are transient and the old wait cost more than the retry; the pause after a 429 is what protects the server.
- **Enrichment (requirement 23.5).** Around a start the app has just loaded streets for, Overpass cells are read from the device cache first; the rest are asked for in the background, one request at a time, at most 2 cells per request, each ending after 12 s, stopping at the first failure or when the client is busy or must wait. Planning never waits for them and never depends on them.
- **Graph cells (requirements 19, 20.10, 21.10).** Fixed 0.01° cells through the same `OverpassClient`, only along the route ahead of the view (at most 6 cells within 1.2 km), one request at a time, at most `GRAPH_BATCH` (2) cells per request, back-off 4 s doubling to 2 min (±25 %), never while offline. Cache first per cell (`v<codec>:cell:i,j`, 7 days, at most `MAX_CELL_ENTRIES` 300); a cache read stuck for 2 s is a miss, a write stuck for 5 s is not saved. A failed graph request changes nothing on screen. Do not add other Overpass prefetching or retries outside this back-off and the enrichment above.
- **Identification.** Browsers send their own Referer and User-Agent. Node scripts must send a descriptive User-Agent with a contact address.
- **Tests and CI never touch the network.** They use the bundled fixture, tiles encoded in the test (`src/vector/testing.ts`) or a mocked `fetch`.

## OpenFreeMap vector tiles (drawing)

OpenFreeMap is free and asks for fair use, caching and the credit below. Rules (`src/vector/client.ts`, `src/world/streamer.ts`):

- **TileJSON at run time.** Read the tile URL from `https://tiles.openfreemap.org/planet` (`TILEJSON_URL`), keep it 24 h on the device, use a stored older one when the read fails. Never write the tileset version (`20261122_124456_pt`-style path) into the code: it changes with every planet build.
- **Two zooms only.** (LOD tiles of requirement 25 are z14 too, see "LOD ring prefetch" below.) z13 for the coarse layer (one tile is about 3.8 km, everything but points of interest) and z14 for detail (about 1.9 km, the highest zoom served; closer zooms reuse it). No other zoom is requested.
- **Only what the screen and the walk need.** Tiles of the cells the camera shows (z14 only above `FAR_ZOOM`) and, with a route, the cells within 1.2 km of the route ahead (at most 6). The one other load is the walking graph of a start whose streets are not on the device (requirement 23): the z14 tiles that meet the cells of one walk (`areaForWalk`; 9 tiles for a 10,000-step loop, 16 at most), cache first, within the 4-in-flight limit below, 15 s a request, two retries (1 s and 2 s ±25 %), 40 s for the whole load. No city download, no prefetch of zooms the view is not at.
- **Cache.** IndexedDB per tile (`v<codec>:mvt:z/x/y`, 7 days, at most `MAX_TILE_ENTRIES` 60, about 50 MB) before any request, plus the browser's HTTP cache (the CDN allows years). A cached tile is drawn at once.
- **Limits.** At most `MAX_TILES_IN_FLIGHT` (4) requests at once: OpenFreeMap's quick start allows downloads "with at most 4 parallel requests and a unique User-Agent" (and up to 3,000 tiles or 50 MB per download, which the view and the route look-ahead stay far below: a cold quest start is about 6 z13 and 6 to 12 z14 tiles). At most 60 starts in any 60 s as a safety net against loops, one request 15 s, the slot freed after 25 s. A failed tile waits 2 s doubling to 60 s (±25 %); a 429 pauses every tile start for 30 s (±25 %); 204 and 404 are an empty tile, never retried. Nothing is sent while the view moves (0.4 s) or offline.
- **Identification.** A web page cannot set `User-Agent` (a forbidden header); the browser sends its own, plus the page's `Referer`, which names StepQuest's origin. A Node script that fetches tiles sends a descriptive User-Agent with a contact address (`StepQuest-…/1.0 (ilias.el.jaafari.el.idrissi@gmail.com)`) and reads only a handful of tiles.
- **Attribution (ODbL, OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles).** The text "OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles © OpenStreetMap contributors" (`OSM_ATTRIBUTION`; `map.creditTiles`, `map.creditSchema` and `map.attribution` in both languages, Spanish "OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles © colaboradores de OpenStreetMap"), each part linked (`https://openfreemap.org`, `https://www.openmaptiles.org/`, `https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright`), is always visible on the map, in the bundled demo and in live data, in the bottom-left corner (requirements 18 and 21.12), on one line at 320 px. Never hide, shrink to unreadable size or move it behind a menu. The bundled extract `src/data/benicalap.osm.json` is ODbL data: keep it credited, and rebuild it only with `npm run fixture`.

## LOD ring prefetch (requirement 25, `src/lod/prefetch.ts`)

Around the hero (the walker, else the planner's start) the app loads the z14 tiles within R (9 km by default, 2 to 15 km with `?lod=<km>`) so the far zooms draw every street and name. That is about 100 tiles at València and about 190 at 60° north, 8 MB on the wire in a measured cold run (dense centre tiles are 400 to 570 KB).

- **Same client, same limit.** It uses `VectorTileClient.loadLod`, so the 4 parallel requests are shared with the map tiles and the walking graph (a fifth is refused as `busy`, and waited for 250 ms, never counted as a failure). Same TileJSON read at run time, same 15 s request and 25 s slot deadlines, same back-off (2 s doubling to 60 s, ±25 %) and 30 s pause after a 429.
- **Order.** Cached tiles are read first (local only). After the view has rested 0.4 s: the tiles the view shows, nearest the view centre first, then the rings by distance from the hero. The ring lane never uses more than 2 slots and waits while a view tile is missing.
- **Caps.** At most 60 ring starts in any 60 s, 200 tiles or 40 MB of raw tile data per session; nothing while offline; rings pause while the page is hidden. The rings pause on `saveData` and `slow-2g`/`2g`, and run one at a time and only to 5 km on `3g`. What the view needs is loaded whatever the caps say.
- **Cache and the CDN.** LOD tiles are kept in IndexedDB (`lod<codec>:z/x/y`, 7 days, at most 320, about 30 MB for a mixed city) and the raw tile stays in the browser's HTTP cache, so a tile that is later needed as detail art is not downloaded twice. The coarse z13 tile is not asked for a cell the LOD layer covers; it is asked again if a LOD tile failed.
- **No other prefetch.** Nothing outside R, nothing for zooms the view is not at.
