# OpenStreetMap, Overpass and OpenFreeMap etiquette

StepQuest runs no server. The map is **drawn** from OpenFreeMap vector tiles (a free CDN, no key) and the **walking graph** comes from public Overpass instances run by volunteers (or the bundled extract). Both are shared, free services, so the client must stay polite. Overpass rules are implemented in `src/osm/overpass.ts` and `src/osm/download.ts`, tile rules in `src/vector/client.ts` and `src/vector/fetchTile.ts`, and both lanes are scheduled by `src/world/streamer.ts`; keep them intact. The reasons for the numbers are in `.kiro/specs/map-rendering/design.md` ("Root cause", "Map loading v2", "Map data from vector tiles"); change a limit only together with that section.

## Overpass (walking graph only)

Never draw from Overpass and never make the drawing wait for it (requirement 21.1): at peak hours the public servers answer 502 "at capacity" or nothing at all.

- **Cache first.** Look in IndexedDB (7-day TTL, `CACHE_TTL_MS`) and in the area index for a cached area that already covers the request before any network call. Cache keys include the codec version and grow the box outwards to a 0.001° grid (about 100 m) so nearby requests share an entry and an entry always covers its key; the grown box is what is downloaded and what the size cap measures. Expired, evicted and older-version entries are deleted locally after a save; this never sends a request. A damaged entry counts as a miss.
- **Two requests at most per tab, spaced.** `MAX_IN_FLIGHT` (2) downloads at once, starts at least `MIN_REQUEST_GAP_MS` (1.5 s) apart, and at most 12 starts in any 60 s (`MAX_REQUESTS_PER_MINUTE`). A third is refused (`busy`), not queued; an explicit "load this area" download runs alone. Why not 1 and 10 s as before: Overpass limits each IP by slots (typically two on the main servers; `/api/status` shows it) and answers 429 when they are taken, so two is what a polite client may hold; one request with a 10 s gap made a 12-cell view take 30 s at best (design.md, Root cause). This doubles the worst-case request rate (6 to 12 a minute) and raises the cells per request from 4 to 6, so the real bound is demand: a cold quest start needs about 6 requests, a 90-minute walk about 10 more, and a second visit nothing for 7 days (cache). Revisit if the mirrors start answering 429 to normal use.
- **Size cap.** Refuse areas above `MAX_AREA_KM2` (36 km²). Ask only for the area a walk needs (`areaForWalk`), and `out geom` for ways only: highways, buildings, parks and green landuse, water, waterways. Relations are not requested (bigger answers); the parser reads multipolygon relations if an extract has them, so adding them to the query is an owner decision.
- **Mirrors, hedged.** The public mirrors in `OVERPASS_ENDPOINTS` are asked in order, the last good one first. The next mirror is asked only when the one before failed or has sent no answer header for `HEDGE_MS` (6 s); the first good header wins and the others are aborted before they send a body (never three bodies over one phone link, never three times the load on volunteers). Limits: header 25 s (`HEADER_TIMEOUT_MS`), stall 12 s (`STALL_TIMEOUT_MS`), one call 60 s (`TOTAL_TIMEOUT_MS`), the client deadline 75 s (`REQUEST_DEADLINE_MS`) that frees the slot and ends the worker. Answer 429 and 504 as `rateLimited`. An answer without an `elements` array is `badResponse` and a timeout remark is `busy`; neither is cached. Do not add mirrors that forbid this use.
- **Back-off with jitter.** A failed graph cell waits 4 s ±25 %, doubling to 2 min; a 429 or 504 pauses every start for at least 20 s ±25 %. Jitter so many phones do not retry together. No unit is given up for good while the view needs it, and a failing unit never delays another. Why shorter than the old 30 s to 5 min: on a mobile link most failures are transient and the old wait cost more than the retry; the pause after a 429 is what protects the server.
- **Graph cells (requirements 19, 20.10, 21.10).** Fixed 0.01° cells through the same `OverpassClient`, only along the route ahead of the view (at most 6 cells within 1.2 km), one request at a time, at most `GRAPH_BATCH` (2) cells per request, back-off 4 s doubling to 2 min (±25 %), never while offline. Cache first per cell (`v<codec>:cell:i,j`, 7 days, at most `MAX_CELL_ENTRIES` 300); a cache read stuck for 2 s is a miss, a write stuck for 5 s is not saved. A failed graph request changes nothing on screen. Do not add other Overpass prefetching or retries outside this back-off.
- **Identification.** Browsers send their own Referer and User-Agent. Node scripts must send a descriptive User-Agent with a contact address.
- **Tests and CI never touch the network.** They use the bundled fixture, tiles encoded in the test (`src/vector/testing.ts`) or a mocked `fetch`.

## OpenFreeMap vector tiles (drawing)

OpenFreeMap is free and asks for fair use, caching and the credit below. Rules (`src/vector/client.ts`, `src/world/streamer.ts`):

- **TileJSON at run time.** Read the tile URL from `https://tiles.openfreemap.org/planet` (`TILEJSON_URL`), keep it 24 h on the device, use a stored older one when the read fails. Never write the tileset version (`20261122_124456_pt`-style path) into the code: it changes with every planet build.
- **Two zooms only.** z12 for the coarse layer (one tile is about 7.5 km) and z14 for detail (about 1.9 km, the highest zoom served; closer zooms reuse it). No other zoom is requested.
- **Only what the screen needs.** Tiles of the cells the camera shows (z14 only above `FAR_ZOOM`) and, with a route, the cells within 1.2 km of the route ahead (at most 6). No bulk prefetch, no area or city download, no prefetch of zooms the view is not at.
- **Cache.** IndexedDB per tile (`v<codec>:mvt:z/x/y`, 7 days, at most `MAX_TILE_ENTRIES` 60, about 50 MB) before any request, plus the browser's HTTP cache (the CDN allows years). A cached tile is drawn at once.
- **Limits.** At most `MAX_TILES_IN_FLIGHT` (6) requests at once (what a browser opens per host anyway), at most 60 starts in any 60 s as a safety net against loops (a full phone view needs about 8), one request 15 s, the slot freed after 25 s. A failed tile waits 2 s doubling to 60 s (±25 %); a 429 pauses every tile start for 30 s (±25 %); 204 and 404 are an empty tile, never retried. Nothing is sent while the view moves (0.4 s) or offline.
- **Identification.** Browsers send their own Referer and User-Agent; a Node script that fetches tiles sends a descriptive User-Agent with a contact address and reads only a handful of tiles.
- **Attribution (ODbL, OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles).** The text "OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles © OpenStreetMap contributors" (`OSM_ATTRIBUTION`; `map.creditTiles`, `map.creditSchema` and `map.attribution` in both languages, Spanish "OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles © colaboradores de OpenStreetMap"), each part linked (`https://openfreemap.org`, `https://www.openmaptiles.org/`, `https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright`), is always visible on the map, in the bundled demo and in live data, in the bottom-left corner (requirements 18 and 21.12), on one line at 320 px. Never hide, shrink to unreadable size or move it behind a menu. The bundled extract `src/data/benicalap.osm.json` is ODbL data: keep it credited, and rebuild it only with `npm run fixture`.
